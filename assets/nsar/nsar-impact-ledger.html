Methodology & caveats

Attribution. "Lives saved directly attributable" and "burnout reduction" are estimates NSAR's search leads and program coordinators should assign case-by-case (e.g., searches where faster location plausibly changed the outcome), not automated counts. State the method next to the number whenever it's shared externally.

SROI, not ROI. Search-and-rescue doesn't generate revenue, so this model reports Social Return on Investment: monetized cost avoidance (shorter searches, fewer volunteer replacements) divided by funding invested. It is a floor on financial value, not a full accounting of impact — the count-based outcomes above it (people assisted, lives saved, wellbeing) are the primary evidence.
