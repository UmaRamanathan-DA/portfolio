Two honesty notes: (1) This prototype excludes gender and marital status from individual-level explanations and intervention logic, even though the underlying model still uses them — a full fairness audit across age, gender, and tenure is a required step before any real deployment, exactly as flagged in the companion decks. (2) These numbers are freshly recomputed live from the uploaded dataset in this session. Headcount totals match the decks exactly (247 stayed / 47 left / 210 not flagged / 84 flagged). The fine-grained confusion-matrix cells differ by exactly one employee each — AUC 0.799 vs. 0.798, recall 59.6% vs. 61.7% in the decks — most likely a minor row-order difference between the dataset copy used here and the original Colab run. Practically identical; disclosed here rather than silently forced to match.

Dataset: IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition (Kaggle) — 1,470 employees, 237 who left / 1,233 who stayed (16.1% attrition), 294 held out for testing. Model: logistic regression, class-weighted, trained on the same 80/20 stratified split used throughout this portfolio. Explanations are computed directly from the model's own coefficients (contribution = standardized feature value × coefficient) — no separate AI layer is used to generate them, so nothing here is guessed or hallucinated. Companion deliverables: case study, product walkthrough deck, results spreadsheet, and two audience-specific briefing decks.
