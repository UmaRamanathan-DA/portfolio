about

I connect human-centred design, quantitative product intelligence, and AI solution design into one coherent product story — not three separate slide decks.

Background spans product design, UX delivery, and data platforms across healthcare, banking, and enterprise software. I work the full lifecycle: field insight and journey mapping, prototyping experiences, measuring behaviour with KPIs and models, scoping where AI should (and should not) automate, architecting integrations for real enterprise systems, and specifying conversational and multi-agent AI with guardrails for high-stakes workflows.

Lately that shows up as hands-on case studies — airline post-disruption recovery agents, compliance field copilots, performance-marketing AI, DORA/DevOps copilots — alongside classic analytics work like demand forecasting, credit risk, and spatial loan dashboards. The through-line is evidence-first product decisions, whether the deliverable is a PRD, a Streamlit prototype, or an agent behaviour spec.

what I do

Most weeks I rotate between discovery (workshops, teardowns, PLG diagnosis), making solutions tangible in Figma or clickable prototypes, and backing bets with data — dashboards, forecasts, risk models, compliance metrics. When the problem fits AI, I map friction to automation boundaries first, then write the product spec: architecture, integrations, human-in-the-loop review, and what “safe autonomy” means in that domain.

Current energy goes into GenAI and agentic workflow case studies (airline IROPS recovery, B2B compliance copilots, ad-tech copilots, DevOps analytics agents), interview-ready PM artefacts, and a personal toolkit of AI-assisted PM skills. I still love the analyst craft — SQL deep dives, interpretable ML, Tableau stories stakeholders actually use — because it keeps product calls honest.
