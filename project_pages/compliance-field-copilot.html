PHASE 3

Portfolio learning exercise — a product specification only, not built or delivered. Builds on Phase 1 (real) and Phase 2 (illustrative).

Field access was solved. Completion still depended on manual template search, typing, and self-checking.

agent architecture diagram

15–20% targeted lift in field activation
20–30% additional targeted reduction in time-to-value

Business Objective

Design an AI layer that helps assessors move faster and with more confidence at the point of inspection, without removing their control over what gets submitted.

Business Challenges

  • Template selection and long-form data entry still slowed assessors down
  • Manual capture kept transcription errors alive even after the redesign
  • Managers had no plain-language way to see where adoption was still breaking down

Approach

Agentic Workflow Design · Voice UX · Product Analytics · Guardrails & Governance

What Changed

  • Designed a smart template recommender Suggests the right template by GPS and history, replacing manual search.
  • Specified voice-to-form capture Assessors speak observations; the copilot maps them to structured fields.
  • Built a pre-submit validation agent Checks completeness before submission — assessor keeps final say.
  • Modeled adoption-risk scoring Flags assessors likely to abandon before they do, not after.
  • Specified a manager insights copilot Plain-language queries over field adoption data, no custom reports needed.

Transformation Scale

7 AI-assisted capabilities specified end-to-end

1 orchestrator coordinating 6 specialised agent tools

4-phase rollout roadmap defined — Foundation → Capture → Intelligence → Admin

A specification for faster, more confident field capture, with a human always in the loop.

Business Impact

  • +15–20% targeted lift in field activation
  • Additional 20–30% targeted reduction in time-to-value beyond Phase 1
  • Abandonment rate targeted to hold at ≤7%, vs. 74% pre-redesign baseline
  • +10% targeted lift in sustained 30–90 day submission rate
  • Full audit trail and human-in-the-loop guardrails specified for every AI suggestion
