Through the Glass Artly: rescued bottles, hand-painted with marine life.

Ksara Decor rescues glass bottles and hand-paints each one into a marine-themed piece — a small, ongoing act of sustainability before it's anything else. It's shaped to grow the same way: as gifts, and as a corporate social responsibility option for businesses sourcing client, staff, or event gifts that actually say something about reducing waste, not just a logo on a mug. Every bottle carries an ocean-conservation thread through its art, so what someone takes home isn't a product so much as a talking piece — a souvenir with a story attached to it.
