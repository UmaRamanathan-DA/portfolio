bobabean, a soulful coffee customization for the vegan brew lovers.

This personal project explores fun UI concepts, Figma prototyping, and product ideation for mobile, hybrid, and tablet platforms. It blends playful design with thoughtful customization mechanics to elevate the coffee experience.


Bobabean is a mobile app concept for a boutique vegan café that celebrates deep coffee customization. Designed to evoke the cozy charm of in-store ordering, the app blends retro aesthetics with tactile UI elements to create a warm, unconventional experience for coffee lovers. This personal project was a playground for playful UI design, Figma prototyping, and strategic feature prioritization.
