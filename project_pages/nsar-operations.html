Product Case · Performance Management Strategy

Arctic search-and-rescue missions are life-or-death — and NSAR had no way to tell which of its training, resourcing, or community efforts actually helped.

This is a Product Strategy Org Building problem.

Academic strategic analysis project completed with the NSAR Project Team at the University of Strathclyde (Oct–Dec 2024), applied to Nunavut Search and Rescue's real Arctic SAR operations.

NSAR had years of SAR intervention data and community knowledge, but no structure linking day-to-day activities to the outcomes they were meant to produce — no way to see where effort was working, or where it wasn't. I designed six pathway-specific logic models, a hierarchical KPI framework, and causal maps connecting inputs to impact — giving NSAR a structure to evaluate performance, not just record it.

Who NSAR leadership and SAR program managers Oversee training, resourcing, and incident response across Arctic search-and-rescue operations.
What See whether activities are producing outcomes Trace a straight line from what NSAR does to what it actually achieves, pathway by pathway.
Why 6 pathways, one connected system Recruitment, training, and incident response each traced from input to impact, not measured in isolation.

Key challenges & solutions

Challenge

Aligning metrics across diverse SAR operational activities.

Solution

Modular logic models tailored to each SAR pathway, instead of one generic scorecard.

Challenge

No centralized performance data.

Solution

An integrated data collection strategy combining community feedback with SAR logs.

Challenge

Balancing traditional knowledge with modern analytics.

Solution

Inuit knowledge embedded directly into environmental-adaptation and training metrics.

Challenge

Anticipating future risk from climate change.

Solution

A proposed Bayesian Network to model environmental variables against SAR readiness.

What we changed

  • Mapped 6 pathways end-to-endLogic models tracing inputs and activities through to outcomes and long-term impact, not a single scorecard.
  • Built a metric hierarchy, not a flat listCore, Inferred, Composite, and Impact metrics, so leverage points are visible instead of buried.
  • Visualized how metrics influence each otherMetric Causal Maps showing the decision-making levers across pathways.
  • Combined two data sources into one strategyPrimary community feedback and secondary SAR logs merged into a single collection plan.

The deliverable wasn't a metric or a dashboard — it was the structure connecting activities to outcomes.
