bobabean, a soul deep coffee customization for the brew lovers.

This personal project explores fun UI concepts, Figma prototyping, and product ideation for mobile, hybrid, and tablet platforms. It blends playful design with thoughtful interaction design to elevate the coffee experience.


Bobabean is a mobile app concept for a boutique vegan café that celebrates deep coffee customization, an experience that true coffee lovers appreciate. Designed to evoke the cozy charm of in-store ordering, the app blends retro aesthetics with tactile UI elements to create a warm, unconventional experience for coffee lovers.

Tablet Experience: BB Cappuccino

BB Cappuccino is bobabean's signature coffee. It is freshly ground with your favorite blend sourced from the best leaves of Africa. With milk of your choice steamed, and some flavor added, you made your own cup of coffee.

Feature Description
Coffee Menu Browse curated vegan coffee blends with rich visuals and flavor notes
Make My Coffee Deep customization: roast, milk, sweetener, toppings, temperature, etc.
Checkout & Review Cart Simple, tactile cart review with animated transitions
Order Confirmation Cozy confirmation screen with café branding and pickup time
Uncollected Order Notification Gentle nudges for forgotten pickups with playful illustrations
