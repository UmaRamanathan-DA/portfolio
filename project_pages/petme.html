Personal Project · Mobile UX · Figma Practice · Rapid Prototyping

Finding a pet takes one swipe. PetMe helps you keep it alive and well with best practices on feeding, grooming and vaccinations.

Pet parenting is scattered by design: adoption listings live on one site, grooming gets booked by phone, and feeding, walks, and vaccinations get tracked nowhere but memory. I designed PetMe to unify discovery, a full daily care schedule, and a real profile per pet into one app — from the adoption swipe to the vaccination reminder.

Personal project · Figma practice · UI concept exploration for pet adoption & care management.

Who Pet parents, before and after People adopting a pet, and people already keeping one alive and well.
What One app, not five systems Discovery, a daily care schedule, and a pet profile, all in one place.
Why A missed reminder isn't minor A forgotten feeding is an inconvenience. A forgotten vaccination is a vet bill.

Problems

  • Adoption listings scattered across sites
  • No single view of a pet's daily care
  • Grooming and vet visits booked by phone
  • No real profile per pet
  • No shared visibility for sitters or family

What today looks like

Adoption is a scavenger hunt

Browsing dogs, cats, and rescues means checking a different site or shelter for each species, with no way to filter by breed or distance in one place.

Care lives in your head

Feeding times, walk schedules, vaccination dates — most of it is memory, a sticky note, or a reminder that never got set.

What I designed

  • Species to specific pet, in one flowDogs → Beagles → "Tough, 1yr, available for free, 10km away" in three taps, not three websites.
  • A full day mapped, not a to-do listFeed, walk, meds, bath, and grooming each sit on an hourly timeline, so a gap in the day is visible, not forgotten.
  • Every pet gets a real profileName, age, birthday, gender — one place that actually answers "how old is she again?"
  • Adoption becomes an ongoing relationship"My Buddies" keeps every adopted pet's posts and updates in view long after the swipe that found them.

A pet doesn't need an app that helps you find it. It needs one that helps you keep it.
