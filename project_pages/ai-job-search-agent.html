An AI agent that automates my daily job search — it checks LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, and Glassdoor every morning, scores each posting against my profile, filters out duplicates from past runs, and saves a ranked shortlist to Google Drive along with an email digest, all without any manual searching. I built it end-to-end using Claude as a single AI agent (no separate tools like Zapier or extra AI models) that runs on a daily schedule, reads its own past results to avoid repeats, and works within real constraints — like sourcing only from public search results (not logins, to respect site rules) and drafting rather than auto-sending emails so a human still reviews before anything goes out. The result: a five-minute morning review replaces what used to be repetitive manual searching across four different job sites.
