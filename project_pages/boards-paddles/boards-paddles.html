🏄 Boards & Paddles

A concept platform for ocean sports enthusiasts

Designed to make discovery, booking, and community engagement effortless and fun.

Created on: Jan 2022

Boards & Paddles is a mobile-first concept app that energizes the ocean sports community by making events, rentals, and reviews more engaging and accessible. It’s built for curious, fun-loving users who crave connection, discovery, and a splash of adventure.

This project explores how digital platforms can foster real-world experiences through intuitive design, thematic storytelling, and community-driven features.

🎯 Goals

  • Unify fragmented ocean sports experiences into one playful platform
  • Encourage exploration through curated packages and rentals
  • Build community via reviews, shared stories, and themed challenges
  • Celebrate ocean culture with a shop for souvenirs and local crafts

🧠 UX Strategy & Research

🔍 Research Process

  • Conducted interviews with ocean sports enthusiasts
  • Mapped emotional highs/lows across typical booking journeys
  • Created empathy maps and personas
  • Sketched low-fidelity wireframes
  • Designed high-fidelity UI in Figma

👤 User Archetypes/Personas

  • Curious First-Timer: Wants to try paddleboarding but feels unsure
  • Local Guide: Hosts events and shares gear tips
  • Family Explorer: Seeks safe, fun group activities
  • Solo Adventurer: Books spontaneous experiences
