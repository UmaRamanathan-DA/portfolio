Credit Risk Prediction

Loan Tap
India
Duration: Aug 2024 – Sep 2024
Academic Project (Scaler)

Project Overview

Developed a machine learning model to predict credit risk and optimize loan approval processes. The system analyzes customer data, credit history, and market trends to assess default risk and streamline loan approval decisions.

Key Responsibilities

• Built predictive models using machine learning algorithms to assess credit risk and default probability

• Analyzed customer data, credit history, and market trends to identify risk factors

• Developed automated loan approval processes to streamline decision-making

• Created risk assessment frameworks to help financial institutions reduce risk while improving customer experience

Methodology & Approach

Utilized supervised learning techniques including Random Forest, Logistic Regression, and Gradient Boosting to build predictive models. Implemented feature engineering to create relevant variables from customer data. Applied cross-validation and hyperparameter tuning to optimize model performance. Used ROC curves and precision-recall metrics to evaluate model effectiveness.

Results & Impact

The credit risk prediction model helped financial institutions reduce default rates while improving customer experience through faster loan approval processes. The automated system enabled more efficient risk assessment and decision-making, leading to better resource allocation and reduced operational costs.
