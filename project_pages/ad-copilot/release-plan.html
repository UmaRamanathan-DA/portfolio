Release Plan · v1.0

A sequence of trust-earning gates, not a launch date

The PRD's three-phase scope, operationalized into entry/exit gates. Each phase expands scope only after the last one earns it.

Owner Product, with Engineering & Design · per-account "pause AI" kill-switch exists from day one.

Three phases

Phase 1
MVP — Creative + Suggest
Meta only. 5–10 design partners, $50K+/mo.
Exit
  • ≥40% acceptance, sustained 4+ weeks
  • Zero brand-safety failures reaching a live account
Phase 2
Multi-platform + budget/bid
+Google Ads. Budget/bid recs, still Suggest-only.
Exit
  • Budget/bid acceptance reaches parity with creative
  • Positive incremental-ROAS trend across cohort
Phase 3
Auto-apply lane
Opt-in only, per campaign, trust-tier eligible accounts.
Success
  • Auto-apply ≥ own Suggest-lane ROAS history
  • Rollback rate low and stable

Feature flags

google_adsOff until Phase 2 entry
budget_bidOff until Phase 2
auto_applyOff until Phase 3, then opt-in per account
ai_pausedPer-account kill-switch, available from Phase 1

Post-launch monitoring

  • Weekly review of guardrails: LLM cost %, acceptance rate, escalation rate.
  • Confidence calibration is ongoing release-health work, not a one-time launch task.
